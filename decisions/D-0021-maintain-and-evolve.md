# Production is not the end: an eighth stage, maintain and evolve

The owner (2026-11-23): "maturity stages include stage of maintenance too, delivery to production is not
final in that sense. Or further dev."

**What this means:**
- **An eighth stage.** After stage 7, deploy and measure, a solution enters stage 8, **maintain and
  evolve**, and stays there while it runs. It holds what already exists: operations rounds, probes and
  incidents, dependency and security updates, drift, update cards for new models, end-user feedback, and
  the change loop that sends a change back through spec, contract, build and validation and returns to
  stage 8 with a new release.
- **Retirement is a person's decision.** It ends stage 8 with the solution's records kept.
- **An adopted solution enters at stage 8** when its intent is maintain.
- **Stage 8 shows its own health.** Open incidents, pending updates, changes in flight, how old the last
  release is, and whether its evidence still holds.
- **Where it lands.** The kernel's stage list, the cockpit's stepper and pipeline, the demo, the docs and
  the design pages carry the eighth stage. Maturity (explore, demo, pilot, production) still says how far
  a solution is trusted; stage 8 says it is in service and still changing.
